# Audio envelope dynamics — separate what is detected from what is controlled

## Activate when

Use this procedure for **attack/release envelopes, transient control, exponential RMS, compressor/expander control curves, dynamics sidechains, detector linking, or analysis of gain-reduction timing**. An envelope is not automatically a meter and is not a prediction of hearing. Use [finite-window RMS](../audio-rms-crest-factor/SKILL.md) for a rectangular RMS window; [sample peak](../audio-sample-peak/SKILL.md) or [true peak](../audio-true-peak/SKILL.md) for clip/headroom claims; [VU](../audio-vu-metering/SKILL.md), [PPM](../audio-ppm-metering/SKILL.md), [programme loudness](../audio-program-loudness/SKILL.md) or [LRA](../audio-loudness-range/SKILL.md) for their particular instruments and programme measures. Do not substitute one for another.

Read the [envelope/dynamics contract](references/ENVELOPE_DYNAMICS_CONTRACT.md) before interpreting or implementing a time constant. Load only relevant [timebase](../audio-timebase-sync/SKILL.md), [numeric stability](../audio-numerical-stability/SKILL.md), [test-fixture](../audio-dsp-test-fixtures/SKILL.md), and [verification](../math-verification/SKILL.md) support.

## Engineering procedure

1. **Declare the signal and units.** Identify processing tap, PCM format/full-scale reference, sample rate, ordered channel labels, exact frame range and epoch. Distinguish signed PCM `x`, magnitude `|x|`, instantaneous power `x²`, smoothed amplitude, exponential mean-square/RMS, level in dBFS, gain reduction in dB, and linear gain. No unannounced DC removal, mono summation, normalisation, or clipping.
2. **Choose the quantity, then the detector.** For sample magnitudes use `|x[n]|`; for exponential RMS smooth `x[n]²` then take the square root. Smooth **after** the nonlinear magnitude/power transform. Rectangular RMS is another procedure; analytic/Hilbert magnitude is an optional offline AM-like diagnostic with FFT boundary assumptions, not a substitute for an A/R sidechain.
3. **Pin the time convention and state domain.** The named `one_e` coefficient (`alpha=exp(-1/(fs*tau))`, `beta=-expm1(-1/(fs*tau))`) defines a **1/e residual-error time for the state being smoothed**: amplitude for `peak`, **mean-square power** `q` for `rms`, and attenuation depth in dB for the gain demonstrator. Peak amplitude rises about 63.212% after one attack tau; RMS amplitude rises about **79.506%**, because `rms=sqrt(q)`. On release to silence, RMS amplitude retains about **60.653%** after one tau, not 36.788%. Likewise `rise_90` and `residual_minus60_db` describe the **filtered state**, not a universal RMS-output amplitude percentage or dB target. Other products may define timing differently. Never transfer a numerical time parameter without its state domain and convention.
4. **Apply explicit attack/release state.** Compare the current nonnegative detector target to the prior nonnegative envelope. Use attack for a higher target, release for a lower target, and `steady` with no state mutation at equality. Update on **each sample**, not once per callback or display frame. `tau=0` means immediate tracking. Initialization is explicit and normally zero. Changing tap, rate, channel layout, or time law begins a new epoch.
5. **State the channel link mode.** `independent` keeps one envelope per declared channel; `max` takes the maximum per-sample magnitude across channels **before** one shared detector. It is not a mono sum and is not the maximum of independent smoothed envelopes. Anti-phase stereo must not cancel. A common gain preserves channel gain ratios in the linked design; independent gains can change the spatial balance.
6. **Keep detection, gain computation, and smoothing distinct.** The bounded hard-knee compressor/expander helpers map *level dBFS → requested gain dB*. A separate gain-reduction follower smooths **positive attenuation depth in dB** and reports a negative applied gain dB and a linear multiplier. Smoothing level before a nonlinear gain curve differs from smoothing requested gain after that curve. State the signal path, threshold, ratio, attack/release law, and whether any gain is actually applied. Hard clipping, a ratio tending to infinity, a sample-peak envelope and a lookahead-less demonstrator do **not** prove true-peak limiter safety.
7. **Preserve time and failure semantics.** Integer sample indexes stay exact beyond `2**53`. Require an exact contiguous start and matching epoch for each push; never invent zero samples for a gap. Validate every supplied sample before committing any part of a callback. Reject NaN, infinity, negative times, invalid ratios and nonrepresentable states without moving the cursor. Treat silence and a tiny finite amplitude differently. Do not clamp to `[-1,1]` without an explicit upstream conversion.
8. **Verify the mathematics independently.** Freeze expected step, release, impulse, RMS-power and gain trajectories before implementing. Probe exact branch equality, neighbouring float values, extreme binary64 magnitudes, time-convention conversions, rate changes, callback partitions, stereo inversion/permutation, stream reset, malformed inputs and large sample coordinates. Use a high-precision or independently derived oracle; do not use the reference helper itself as the only source of expected results. See [frozen fixture ledger](../../tests/fixtures/AUDIO_ENVELOPE_DYNAMICS_GOLDENS.md) and [targeted tests](../../tests/test_audio_envelope_dynamics_reference.py).

## Reference and use limits

The [Python reference](scripts/reference_envelope_dynamics.py) provides peak and exponential-RMS A/R followers, independent or maximum channel linking, hard-knee gain functions, and a separate gain-reduction demonstrator. It is **offline educational code** using per-sample output tuples and whole-callback validation. It is not a realtime host backend, sidechain processor, mastering compressor, noise gate, lookahead limiter, IEC/EBU-compliant meter, or proof of audible transparency. It has **zero modeled lookahead** and reports observations on the input sample clock; gain curves are not applied to PCM. This skill contains no audio hardware, true-peak ceiling, psychoacoustic calibration, or measured agent-efficacy claim.

These are evidence of design choices and different conventions, **not** a universal generic-envelope compliance standard.
